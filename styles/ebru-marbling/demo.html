The method

Four steps, one sheet, no second chances.

Every pattern in our shop starts the same way. The water is thickened with gum tragacanth (kitre), the pigments are ground with a drop of ox-gall, and the paper only touches the surface once.

Kitre

Thicken the water until a drop of paint floats instead of sinking.

Damla

Flick pigment from a rose-stem brush. Each drop pushes the others aside.

Tarak

Pull a comb through the surface to turn spots into gelgit waves.

Kâğıt

Lay the paper down, lift it once, and dry it flat. The tray is empty again.
