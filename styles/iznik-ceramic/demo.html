How we make them

Your design, through four hands and one kiln.

These four stages always show the tile you designed above. Change the design and the drawings change with it.

Step 1

Desen

The pattern is drawn once on paper and pricked along every line.

Step 2

Silkme

Charcoal dust is tapped through the holes onto the white slip.

Step 3

Tahrir & boya

Outlines in dark pigment, then cobalt, turquoise and the raised red.

Step 4

Fırın

A clear glaze and one firing at 900 °C. The red stands slightly proud.
