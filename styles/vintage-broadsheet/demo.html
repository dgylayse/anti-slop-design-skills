The waterfront

Old ferry pier to reopen as a public reading room

Council votes to keep the 1908 pier in public hands; repairs to begin after the spring tides.

By M. Oyelaran · City desk

After three years behind a fence, the ferry pier at the foot of Wharf Street will open again — this time with shelves. The council voted on Tuesday to lease the waiting hall to the city library for a nominal sum.

Engineers say the timber piles are sound below the waterline; the decking above them is not. Repairs are expected to take until late summer.
