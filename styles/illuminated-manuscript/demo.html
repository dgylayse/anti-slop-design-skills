    Here followeth · our rules

    How we transcribe, line by line.

    i.

    We keep the spelling

    “As written” shows the scribe's own spelling. “Modern” is a reading aid, never a replacement.

    ii.

    We bracket doubt

    Where a word is worn or uncertain, we write it in [square brackets] rather than guess.

    iii.

    We note every hand

    Marginal notes, later corrections and the occasional doodle are transcribed beside the text.

    The reading room

    See the leaves themselves.

    Researchers and curious readers may book a two-hour place. Pencils only; we provide cradles and weights.
