Here beginneth · the collection

Two hundred leaves of vellum, read closely.

ach folio in this archive was photographed under daylight and raking light, then transcribed line by line by our volunteers. Read the hand as the scribe wrote it, or switch to a plain modern transcription. Marginal notes, corrections and the occasional doodle of a cat are kept, because they are part of the book. Rubrics in red mark where a new text begins; gold marks the feasts and the names the makers thought worth honouring. If a word is uncertain, we say so in square brackets rather than guess. Researchers may book the reading room; everyone else can turn the pages here, with the zoom, at any hour.
