Set · Ink · Pull

Set the type

Letters are picked from the case and locked into the forme, one line at a time.

Ink the forme

A roller passes over the type. Too much ink and the letters fill in; too little and they go grey.

Pull the print

The platen presses paper onto inked type. Every pull is a little different.
