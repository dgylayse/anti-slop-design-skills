# Composing Skills

1. Select one primary `styles/<name>/SKILL.md`; apply its structure, geometry, type and imagery rules.
2. Optional: select one `palettes/<name>/SKILL.md`; override **only** `--color-*` semantic variables after the style variables.
3. Choose one `layouts/<name>/SKILL.md` or one `social-media/<name>/SKILL.md` for structural delivery.
4. `meta/design-director` can recommend those choices and review their outputs. Do not substitute its generic guidance for the chosen concrete skill.
5. Build responsive semantic HTML or platform-native accessible components; test all states and contrast.

The historical period label is a conceptual design reference. Do not reproduce period artworks or living designers' proprietary work. Demo artwork is original CSS/SVG illustration.
