## Prompt

```text
Use design-director, [industry layout], [visual style] and [optional palette].
Briefly define brand identity, user goal, IA and mobile order before coding.
Avoid generic AI hero/card patterns. Build accessible keyboard states and
real task flows; do not invent reviews, bookings, prices or credentials.
```
